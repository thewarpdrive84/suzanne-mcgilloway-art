'Fournier Street, Spitalfields' Featured In 'Spitalfields Life'

I'm delighted to get a mention in the latest 'Spitalfields Life' blog by the Gentle Author. I'm a fan and follower of the blog so was touched to have my oil painting included. I'm excited to exhibit 'Fournier Street, Spitalfields' alongside these artists at Town House Spitalfields. The exhibition launches on Saturday 12th September.
